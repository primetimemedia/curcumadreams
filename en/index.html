Interiors with feeling

Every room gets its own character.

Good interiors are all about the interplay of furniture, colours, fabrics and materials. Often it is the small details that make a room special – even if it is just changing a cushion.

We use natural materials. The style is modern, classic or Mediterranean, in a loft or Scandi look with interesting shapes.

Our aim is to translate our clients' taste and wishes into an atmosphere where they truly feel at home.

Sometimes a calm, natural interior is just right.
And sometimes a little more colour is welcome.

About us

Experience and a love of interiors.

Behind Curcuma Dreams Interior is a small team with many years of experience in interior furnishing.

It all started in Germany, where for many years we furnished homes, holiday apartments, hotels and restaurants.

Today we also work in Mallorca, combining our experience with Mediterranean living and the particular requirements of properties on the island.

In our store in Campos we are happy to advise you in person and show you a selection of furniture, fabrics and home accessories.

If you want to furnish an entire property, we accompany you from planning to the finished interior. We take care of selecting the furniture, ordering and installation on site.

What matters to us is that you feel well advised and end up with an interior you will enjoy for a long time.

YEARS OF EXPERIENCE

We have been working with furniture, fabrics and interiors for many years. This experience helps us – and you – find the right solutions for different rooms and requirements.

GERMANY / INTERNATIONAL

Thanks to our experience in Germany and internationally with different manufacturers, materials and furnishing options, our clients benefit during selection and implementation.
